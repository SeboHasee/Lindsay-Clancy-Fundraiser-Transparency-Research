# Contributing

## How to help

- Submit authoritative sources.
- Report data errors with evidence.
- Report source changes.
- Suggest methodology improvements.
- Provide verification evidence for organizations/public figures.

## Required standards

- Provide provenance and source citations.
- Distinguish fact from inference.
- Do not submit deanonymization content.
- Do not submit private personal data.

## Review policy

Community submissions are not treated as facts automatically.
They are triaged and reviewed before any dataset change is accepted.
